# Image sources

All photography used on the ReformaCasa Valencia demo site is real stock
photography sourced from Unsplash, downloaded once and stored locally under
`src/assets/` (no hotlinking, no runtime dependency on Unsplash). Every photo
below is published under the **Unsplash License** (free to use for
commercial and non-commercial purposes, no permission or attribution
required — https://unsplash.com/license). Attribution is recorded here for
provenance/record-keeping only; it is not displayed on the site, since the
license doesn't require it.

None of these depict a real ReformaCasa Valencia project — see the
gallery/testimonials sections' own copy, which already discloses that the
projects and reviews are illustrative demo content, not real clients.

## Note on the before/after pair

`campanar-bano-before.jpg` and `campanar-bano-after.jpg` are two different
bathrooms from two different photographers, not before/after shots of the
same physical room — genuine matched before/after photography of one room
doesn't exist as generic stock photography. They were chosen because they
read as a convincing, honest "dated → renovated" transformation (worn tile
and fixtures vs. a contemporary stone-and-black-fixture finish), which is
what the block's UI needs; this mirrors how the gallery's project
descriptions are already disclosed as illustrative/demo content rather than
real ReformaCasa Valencia work.

## Superseded assets

The procedurally-generated placeholder SVG illustrations used before this
pass (architectural line-art) were removed from `src/assets/` and are not
referenced by `src/config/site.ts` anymore.
