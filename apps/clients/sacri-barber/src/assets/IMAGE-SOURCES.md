# Image sources

All photography used on the Sacri Barber demo site is real stock
photography sourced from Unsplash, downloaded once and stored locally under
`src/assets/` (no hotlinking, no runtime dependency on Unsplash). Every photo
below is published under the **Unsplash License** (free to use for
commercial and non-commercial purposes, no permission or attribution
required — https://unsplash.com/license). Attribution is recorded here for
provenance/record-keeping only; it is not displayed on the site, since the
license doesn't require it.

None of these depict a real Sacri Barber location or client — see the
gallery/testimonials sections' own copy, which already discloses that the
work shown and reviews are illustrative demo content, not real clients.

## Note on the before/after pair

`gallery/barba-antes.jpg` and `gallery/barba-despues.jpg` are two different
people from two different photographers, not before/after shots of the same
client — genuine matched before/after photography of one person doesn't
exist as generic stock photography (same reasoning already documented in
apps/clients/reforma-casa-valencia's IMAGE-SOURCES.md for its bathroom
before/after pair). The gallery copy for this item avoids claiming the
"before" photo shows a neglected beard — it's simply a moodier portrait, not
visibly unkempt — and instead frames both photos as "two moments," so the
copy doesn't overclaim what the images actually show.
